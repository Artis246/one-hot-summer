One Hot Summer - Help

Summer 1990 on Pickwick Lake: Gavin tubes, skis, and finally gets up on Dad's old wooden slalom ski.

How to steer

Hold your phone in landscape and tilt it like a steering wheel. Tilt and hold to lean into a turn; level it to straighten out. If steering feels off, hold the phone the way you like to play, pause, and tap "reset tilt". Playing lying down, or tilting is hard? In the pause menu, "steer with" switches between tilt, small tilts and a thumb drag.

Getting up out of the water

Follow the hint on screen when the boat starts to pull - every start can be won by feel.

High scores

As your summer goes on, the score games unlock (Tube Hang-On, Wake Jam, Buoy Run, Trick Run). They post to Game Center if you're signed in. Each week has a fresh board that starts Monday morning. If you're offline, your score waits on your device and goes up when you're back online; a weekly score has to reach Game Center before its week ends, or it's dropped (your all-time best still counts).

Saving pictures

Mom's Polaroids can be saved to Photos or shared. Saving to Photos asks permission the first time (the game can only add pictures, never read your library). The game never records your screen, camera or microphone.
